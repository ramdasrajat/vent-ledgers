Plausibility read-out

Method, in brief
Random-forest models trained per metabolic pathway on ThermoBase organisms' own temperature, pH, and oxygen tolerance data predict which pathways could survive the chosen conditions. A literature-derived mapping table then translates surviving pathways into candidate gases and flags how easily each gas could also form abiotically (e.g. methane via serpentinization, H₂S via volcanic outgassing).
Limitations
ThermoBase covers thermophiles only — conditions outside its observed range (40–106°C, pH 0.7–9.5) return no prediction rather than a guess. Pathway probabilities are correlational, drawn from a single dataset, and do not model reaction thermodynamics directly.
